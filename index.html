Employment Ministry of Rural Development

🛠️ MGNREGA

Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme. Guarantees 100 days of wage employment per rural household. Evaluates wage rate shifts, days guaranteed (100–200 days), and accidental death/disability insurance cover for worker families.

Baseline: 100 Days/yr Target: Rural Landless Budget: ₹86,000 Cr
Agriculture Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

🧑‍🌾 PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Direct income support scheme providing ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments to farmer families. Evaluates cash transfer hikes (₹6k–₹15k/yr), small farmer interest subvention, and MSP procurement adjustments across states.

Baseline: ₹6,000/yr Target: Small & Marginal Farmers DBT Direct Transfer
Food Security Ministry of Consumer Affairs & Food

🌾 PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana

Free foodgrain distribution to over 80 crore beneficiaries under NFSA. Simulates grain entitlement increases (5–20 kg/month), pulses inclusion, and targeted nutritional fortification for SC/ST and BPL households.

Baseline: 5 kg/person/mo Target: BPL & SC/ST Families Free Foodgrains
Healthcare National Health Authority (NHA)

🏥 Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

World's largest government-funded healthcare assurance scheme. Models secondary and tertiary hospital cover expansion (₹5L–₹10L), outpatient medicine subsidies, and maternal healthcare benefits across low-resource districts.

Baseline: ₹5 Lakh Cover Target: 50 Cr Beneficiaries Cashless Hospitalization
Housing & Infra Ministry of Rural Development

🏠 PM Awas Yojana (Gramin)

Provides financial assistance for constructing pucca houses with hygienic cooking spaces, electricity, and clean tap water connections. Simulates women co-ownership incentives, solar rooftop subsidies, and unit assistance adjustments.

Baseline: ₹1.2L per Unit Target: Rural Homeless Women Empowerment

Core Methodology & 4-Layer Architecture

Engineered for high-rigor, auditable AI decision support without empirical hallucination

1

GraphRAG Policy Knowledge Graph

One-time LLM extraction synthesizing relationships between Indian policy levers, line ministry schemes, demographic cohorts, and outcome indicators into a structured knowledge network.

2

Causal Inference Engine

Applies Difference-in-Differences (DiD) and EconML Causal Forests to separate genuine policy treatment effects from background macroeconomic trends and state-level confounding variables.

3

Monte Carlo & PPO RL Optimizer

1,000 stochastic simulation iterations generate empirical confidence bands (95% CI). A Stable-Baselines3 PPO agent navigates the multi-objective Pareto frontier under strict fiscal expenditure constraints.

4

FastAPI & Llama-3.3-70B Advisory

High-performance REST API executing state simulations in milliseconds, coupled with Groq Llama-3.3-70B for executive synthesis, fiscal risk warnings, and strategic ministry recommendations.

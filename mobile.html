Government Schemes & Welfare Programs

Access and simulate major Government of India central schemes directly in the AI Policy Simulator.

Employment & Rural Official Portal ↗

MGNREGA

Ministry of Rural Development

Guarantees 100 days of wage-employment per financial year to rural households whose adult members volunteer for unskilled manual work.

Target: Rural Landless & Marginal Households
Budget: ₹86,000 Cr
Agriculture & Farmers Official Portal ↗

PM-KISAN

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Provides direct income support of ₹6,000 per annum in three equal installments directly into bank accounts of landholding farmer families across India via DBT.

Target: Small & Marginal Farmer Families
Budget: ₹60,000 Cr
Food Security Official Portal ↗

PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana

Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

Distributes free foodgrains (5 kg per person per month) to over 81.35 crore beneficiaries covered under the National Food Security Act (NFSA).

Target: Antyodaya & Priority (BPL/SC/ST) Households
Budget: ₹2,05,000 Cr
Healthcare & Insurance Official Portal ↗

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

National Health Authority & Ministry of Health

Provides ₹5 Lakh annual health cover per family for secondary & tertiary hospitalization to over 12 crore poor families.

Target: Bottom 40% Deprived & Vulnerable Population
Budget: ₹7,500 Cr
Housing & Infrastructure Official Portal ↗

PM Awas Yojana (Gramin)

Ministry of Rural Development

Assists rural homeless families in constructing pucca houses with piped water, electricity, and clean cooking gas.

Target: Houseless & Kutcha House Inhabitants
Budget: ₹54,000 Cr
Drinking Water Official Portal ↗

Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM)

Ministry of Jal Shakti

Providing functional tap water connection to every rural household with 55 litres per capita per day.

Target: Rural Households Across All States
Budget: ₹70,000 Cr
